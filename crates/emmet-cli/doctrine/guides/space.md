# Space
Also: sci-fi, scifi, science fiction, spaceship, starship, starfighter, space station, moon base, rover, rocket

Spaceships, stations, rovers, and bases, from figure-scale playsets to display models.

## Rules

- One main color, a second color for panel lines in 10 to 20% of the seams, one accent for engines and markings:
  light bluish gray with dark gray or tan lines and trans-light blue glow; or white with blue and trans-yellow.
- Surfaces mostly smooth. Detail comes from panel edges and a few bare-stud patches, about 70% smooth to 30% texture;
  greebles cluster at engines, joints, and undersides (`bare_stud_greeble`, `greeble_strip`, `angled_hull_panel`).
- Hull shape: `wedge_wing`, `angled_hull_panel`, `stepped_curve_hull` for a curved wall, `stave_cylinder` for a round
  section 6 to 12 studs across; `engine_nozzle`, `cockpit_canopy`, `landing_leg`, `turret`, `radar_dish`,
  `antenna_mast`, `solar_panel`, `glow_trim_edge` for glow strips.
- A figure-scale ship keeps its cockpit figure-sized and compresses the rest to about a third of its length.
- Rooms: black or dark gray tiled floors, smooth dark panel walls with a few pillars and lit panels, doors 4 wide and
  5 bricks tall with cut corners in walls 2 studs thick, corridors 4 to 6 wide, rooms 6 to 10 deep and open at the
  back (`chamfered_door_portal`, `hangar_chevron_floor`, `recessed_glow_panel`, `lit_window_panel`,
  `checker_light_wall`, `pipe_column_cluster`, `control_console`, `hatch_on_hinge`).
- One play feature a room: an elevator, a sliding wall, a hinged hull section, a pod.
- A display spaceship comes with a black stand that puts it at 10 to 20 degrees, and a plaque (`smooth_display_base`).
- Moon and planet bases: gray ground with craters (round plates and tiles), a `landing_zone`, `lattice_tower`s and
  `bar_truss_brace`s, a `round_deck_dome`.
