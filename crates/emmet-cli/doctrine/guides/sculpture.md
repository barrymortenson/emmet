# Sculpture
Also: sculptures, statue, bust, character, characters, brick-built figure, animal, animals, letters, word, sign, mosaic, object

Characters, animals, objects, and big letters built as solid shapes: a smooth skin on a hidden core, judged by its
silhouette.

## Rules

- Silhouette first. Get the outline right from the front and the side before any detail: build the core and the side
  contours first, then the skin.
- A hidden core with studs in every direction (`snot_core`), and on it the skin. Inside it can be any color.
- Few studs showing. Every visible top edge gets a curved slope and every underside an inverted one, corners get
  quarter domes, flat tops get tiles (`curved_slope_skin`, `layered_plate_cap`). Studs show only where the real thing
  has texture or where something attaches.
- Round forms by size: under 4 studs, special parts (domes, round bricks); 4 to 8 studs, quarter pieces and curved
  slopes on a stepped stack (`dome_from_quarters`, `rounded_column`); over 8, slabs shaped from plates and stood up on
  the core (`contour_slab`, `sphere_from_caps`).
- Two to four colors outside. A color changes on a piece's edge, never across a brick face; logos and stripes are flat
  mosaics laid on studs-out walls. A light belly under a dark back (`countershade_belly`).
- Cute means a big head: 40 to 50% of a cartoon character's height, eyes low and close, a simple face
  (`side_stud_eyes`, `big_round_eye`, `muzzle_and_nose`, `teeth_row`, `floppy_ear`).
- Joints where it should pose: a ball for a neck, hinges and clips for limbs (`posable_neck_ball`, `hinged_limb`,
  `ball_joint_arm`).
- Feathers, scales, and fur are shingled tiles or slopes (`feather_shingles`).
- Sculptures are hollow: a 12-stud ball is about 300 parts, a 10-stud head about 150.

## Letters and words

`brick_letters` builds a word: letters 9 bricks tall, strokes 2 studs wide and 2 deep, diagonals as stair steps capped
with slopes, a 1-stud outline behind in a contrast color.

## Presenting it

A display base 2 plates thick and 2 to 4 studs bigger than the model all round, smooth edges, a nameplate tile on the
front (`smooth_display_base`). Something that flies or floats stands on a clear rod above its base
(`hover_stand`). A head or bust goes on a pedestal at least a third of its height (`pyramid_pedestal`),
with a Technic spine inside.
