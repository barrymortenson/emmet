# Pirate
Also: pirates, ship, ships, sailing ship, harbor, island, shipwreck, tavern

Sailing ships, harbors, islands, and the taverns on their rocks, at figure scale.

## Rules

- A ship is compressed: about 16 studs wide and 40 to 60 long, two decks of 6 to 7 bricks each, still with a figure's
  door, cabin, and wheel (`ship_hull`, `plank_deck`, `mast_and_yard`, `crows_nest`, `rudder_hinge`, `cannon_port`,
  `portholes`, `oars`).
- The hull is dark below and curves: reddish brown or black with a contrasting band at the gunwale, a rounded stern
  and a pointed bow; never a plain box.
- Decks are planks: reddish brown or dark tan tiles, hatches, a capstan (`crank_winch`), barrels, crates.
- The harbor is wood on stone: a `dock_pier` on rock, a tavern with a `plank_floor`, a bar (`bar_counter`), a
  fireplace, a bottle shelf, `lamp_post`s.
- Islands: sand, palms, rocks in two grays, surf at the edge (`palm_tree`, `rock_outcrop`, `surf_line`, `cave_mouth`).
- Palette: reddish brown and dark brown wood, tan sand, black and dark red for the ship's trim, white or tan sails,
  pearl gold for treasure, blue and trans-blue water.
- Play: the hull opens (a side panel or lift-off deck), cannons fire, a hidden cave holds treasure.
