# Dinosaur park
Also: dinosaur, dinosaurs, dino, jungle, safari, zoo, wildlife park

Jungle parks with big animals: gates, fences, labs, jeeps, and creatures built from bricks.

## Rules

- The jungle is dense: four or five greens plus olive, plants touching, no base stud showing within 4 studs of a
  wall. Layer it: tall trees behind, palms and ferns in the middle, low leaves in front (`palm_tree`, `bamboo_grove`,
  `tree`, `bush`, `layered_conifer`).
- Earth in reddish brown and dark brown, rock in dark bluish gray, water dark and still (`river`, `waterfall`,
  `rock_outcrop`, `cliff_wall`).
- The built park is concrete and timber: gray or tan gates with heavy pylons, tall fences on posts (`fence`,
  `bolted_pillar`), signs, lamps, a control room inside a pylon, open at the back.
- Big animals are sculptures: a core with studs in every direction, a skin of curved slopes, dark above and light
  below, tooth plates for jaws, sections tapering to the tail; posable on hinges and balls (`guide sculpture`:
  `snot_core`, `curved_slope_skin`, `countershade_belly`, `teeth_row`, `hinged_limb`).
- Vehicles are rugged 6-wide off-roaders and trucks: fender flares, roll bars, light bars (`offroad_fender_flare`,
  `light_bar`, `guide vehicles`).
- Play: a gate that opens, a fence an animal breaks through, a lab with a hatching egg.
