# Fantasy
Also: magic, magical, fairy tale, storybook, whimsical, elven, hill house

Storybook worlds: houses in hills, crooked towers, magic gardens, wizards' rooms. Built like town and medieval models,
but with curves where they'd have corners and color where they'd be plain.

## Rules

- Curves beat corners. At least the base's outline and one main opening are curved: a round door (a disc door
  on a hinge inside a ring of quarter-circle parts), a `round_window_frame`, rounded grass edges, curved walls (`curved_wall_end`, `organic_ground_outline`).
- No straight base edge longer than 6 studs: break it with a round-corner plate or a step.
- Buildings sit in the land, not on it: a hill or rock pushes the floor up 1 to 2 bricks, turf rolls over the roof
  (`contour_terraces`, `hillside`, `bermed_wall_reveal`, `grass_edge`).
- Old stone shows its studs: rough walls of plates, tiles, and bare studs mixed (`exposed_stud_stonework`,
  `irregular_stone_wall`), on a ledge of rough stone (`rough_stone_ledge`).
- An earth palette with one strong accent per area: greens, tan and dark tan, reddish brown, two grays for rock, then a
  dark green door, a red rug, a lavender roof.
- Plants climb everything: vines and flowers on walls and rocks, one twisted tree as the centerpiece (`gnarled_tree`,
  `blossom_tree`, `flower_bed`, `potted_plant`). About one small detail every 4 to 6 studs of wall.
- Towers lean on taper, not on height: `tapered_tower_cap`, `pyramid_spire_tower`, `round_tower`, with a crooked
  chimney.
- Rooms are cozy and open at the back: a hearth, a dresser, a round table, a short four-post bed, shelves of jars
  (`hearth_fireplace`, `dresser_shelf`, `figure_bed`, `wall_shelf`).
- Magic is color and light: trans-clear and trans-colored parts as crystals, glowing windows, a pond with lilies
  (`lily_and_reeds`).

## Scenes

A scene wider than about 32 studs is built as modules on their own bases, joined by plates or pins, each module's
steps grouped together. Mix scales for depth: figure-scale rooms in front, micro houses on the hill behind.
