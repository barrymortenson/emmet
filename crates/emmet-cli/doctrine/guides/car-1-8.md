# Car 1:8
Also: 1:8, 1 8, car 18, supercar, technic, technic car, technic scale

A large car of Technic parts, about 1:8 to 1:14: a frame of liftarms, panels pinned on, and working mechanisms. Every
function it shows is real and connected.

## Rules

- Start with the frame: a ladder of liftarms and Technic frames, the two rails 7 to 9 holes apart
  (`liftarm_ladder_frame`). Build the mechanisms as modules and pin them into it.
- Function first: the steering wheel turns the front wheels through a rack (`steering_rack`); the wheels hang on
  suspension (`wishbone_suspension`); the rear wheels turn the engine's pistons through gears (`v_engine`,
  `gear_pair`, `worm_drive`). If you can't connect a function, leave it out rather than fake it.
- The body is Technic panels pinned at three or more points each, the frame showing below; body lines come from the
  panels' edges. Gaps between panels are normal.
- Doors and the bonnet go on pins on the frame and open (`front_hinged_bonnet`, `technic_pin_hinge`).
- Colored pins and axles are fine: they read as Technic. Body panels in one color, the frame black or dark gray.
- Figure-scale details are far too small here: lights are 2-stud rings on pin holes, the grille a wide tile between
  them (`ring_light_grille`), the wheels 5 to 8 studs across on hubs.
- Size: a supercar about 60 holes long; a smaller Technic car 30 to 45. 1,000 to 3,000 parts: ask before going past
  2,000 (the doctrine).

A system-brick display car (studs and tiles over a Technic skeleton) is `guide large`, not this.
