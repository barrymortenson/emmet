# Modern
Also: contemporary, glass, minimalist, office, villa, skyscraper

Contemporary buildings: glass, flat roofs, clean lines, and few colors.

## Rules

- Few colors, large fields: white, light gray, or tan walls with dark gray or black trim, wood (reddish brown or
  medium nougat) as the one warm accent, trans-clear or trans-black glass.
- Clean lines: smooth tiled faces, no wall textures, no ornament; long horizontal runs of window; flat roofs with a
  thin parapet, or a shallow single pitch.
- Big glass: window walls of 1 x 4 x 3 frames or glass panels side by side, a sliding door; at High detail, mullions
  of thin tiles over glass (`snot_panel`, `bracket_side_panel` for the smooth faces).
- Cantilevers and offsets: an upper floor that overhangs the one below, a carport, a deck (`jettied_floor`,
  `lift_off_floor`).
- Shallow, flat, or green roofs: a roof terrace with a rail, planters, solar panels (`solar_panel`, `potted_plant`,
  `balustrade_parapet`).
- Landscaping is ordered: rectangles of lawn and paving, a pool of trans tiles, clipped hedges, slim trees
  (`flagstone_paving`, `clipped_hedge`, `cypress`, `tiled_pond_water`).
- Interiors are open-plan: one big room, a kitchen island, a long sofa, few objects.
