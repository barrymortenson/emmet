## Decisions that shape it (2026-10-28, docs/decisions.md)

- **D-48: techniques aren't derived from OMR models.** The 43 corpus models are all CC BY 2.0, which would allow
  derived snippets with credit, but Barry chose to use the mining only to rank ideas. Each technique is written from
  general building knowledge and Barry's instruction books (sets 21335, 10350, 43217, 60440, read for methods only,
  never copied into the repo), and its provenance note says so. ADR 0003 records it.
- **D-49: no rung 2 eval exists yet** (`emmet eval` runs rung 1; rung 2 comes with 5A). Done when 2 is proved by
  what exists, and the real rung 2 scores move to 5A and G2.

## Order of work

1. ADR 0003's section on mined techniques; D-48 and D-49 in plan section 03 and section 21's 3C text.
2. The miner, `tests/it/src/bin/mine-techniques.rs`: each corpus model's connection graph (`check::links`),
   connected sub-graphs of 2–12 parts grown from links that aren't plain stud-on-top stacking, keyed by shape (part
   ids and their placement relative to an anchor, color ignored), ranked by models × instances.
3. The research notes from the instruction books (outside the repo), matched to the mined shapes.
4. `scripts/technique-candidates`: review pages `docs/techniques/candidates/<batch>.html`, 20 candidates each, with
   a render, frequency, example models, the books' set numbers, and a proposed name and parameters. Barry accepts or
   rejects each (H-22).
5. Each accepted candidate becomes a technique in `data/techniques/` (3B's format plus a `PROVENANCE` note), with
   its parts added to ldraw-mini, a render, and a test. `emmet technique` returns the provenance.
6. Doctrine: when to reach for which technique, in `crates/emmet-cli/doctrine/EMMET.md`.
