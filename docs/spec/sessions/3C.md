## Decisions that shape it (2026-10-28, docs/decisions.md)

- **D-48: techniques aren't derived from OMR models.** The corpus models are CC BY 2.0, which would allow derived
  snippets with credit, but Barry chose to use the mining only to rank ideas. Each technique is written from general
  building knowledge and Barry's instruction books (sets 21335, 10350, 43217, 60440, read for methods only, never
  copied into the repo), and its provenance note says so. ADR 0003 records it.
- **D-49: no rung 2 eval exists yet** (`emmet eval` runs rung 1; rung 2 comes with 5A). Done when 2 is proved by
  what exists, and the real rung 2 scores move to 5A and G2.
- **Review pages show our own sketches.** Crops of official models where a candidate's key parts meet often showed
  them used for something else, so each candidate is a sketch in the DSL, validated and rendered.
- **Every kind of build, not only houses** (Barry, after batch 1): batches by type, vehicles and trains, landscape
  and nature, castles and ships, sci-fi and mechanisms; the corpus widened to every theme.
- **The cottage follows the techniques** (Barry): walls in bond with interlocking corners, a staggered roof.

## What was built

1. ADR 0003's section on mined techniques; D-48 and D-49 in plan section 03 and section 21's 3C text.
2. Research: four agents read Barry's instruction books (88 techniques and their step lessons, kept in `/tmp`, not the
   repo); their part ids were checked against the library.
3. The miner, `tests/it/src/bin/mine-techniques.rs`, on the validator's connection graph (`check::joints`): motifs
   of 2–12 parts keyed by shape, counted by set, and `--query` for how widely sets join a candidate's key parts. It
   read 1,318 sets (`docs/techniques/corpus.json`, fetched with `scripts/omr-corpus <dir> docs/techniques/corpus.json`).
4. `scripts/technique-candidates`: five review pages, `docs/techniques/candidates/batch-1.html` to `batch-5.html`,
   20 candidates each with a sketch (`batch-N/<name>.py`), its render, frequency, books, and notes. Batches 3 to 5 were
   sketched by three agents in parallel and every sketch rerun here before Barry saw it.
5. 98 accepted techniques in `data/techniques/` (118 in all), each with a `PROVENANCE` note, a render, a test, and its
   parts in ldraw-mini; `emmet technique` and MCP `technique` return the provenance.
6. `m.wall` lays courses in bond over the course below's joints (`Family.bond`); the cottage template is rebuilt to it.
7. Doctrine: "Build like a designer", and every technique named by what it's for.
8. Fixes found on the way, each with a test: LDraw files with a byte order mark (Rust parser and `import_ldraw`).

## Not done here

- The validator reports a collision between click-hinge bricks (30540/30541) at any angle but 0 (hinge plates pass):
  a likely false positive, found while sketching; an issue with Barry's OK.
- Mudflaps, train bogies, buffer beams, car doors, sliding doors, and racks have no connector data, so they can't
  validate as held; no technique uses them.
- round_tower's seams line up course to course and ship_hull's bow wedges are tied only by the deck (Barry accepted
  them as written); wheel_axle and wheel_arch were rejected (aircraft wheels, not a car's).
