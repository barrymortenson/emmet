## What gets built → how

1. **`docs/spec/test-matrix.md`**: a table per plan section (14's flows, states, and keys; 09's commands, tools, and
   error codes; 11's rules; 15's controls). Each row names the behavior, the test file, the test's name, and the OS.
   `tests/scripts/test-matrix.test.mjs` reads it and fails on a row with no test, a file that doesn't exist, a test
   name that isn't in its file, or a file CI doesn't run (not in an E2E tier, not in `tests/it`'s one binary, not
   under a `just test` suite). Gaps found while writing it get a test at the highest level that reaches them.
2. **The replay corpus**: at least 15 scrubbed transcripts per agent across rungs 1 to 3, with real failures and
   recoveries. Rung 1 and rung 2 are there from 1D and 4B; rung 3's, and the failures, come from Barry's H-09
   sessions through `scripts/scrub-transcript`, each replayed by `tests/it/tests/agent_{claude,codex}.rs`. A test
   counts them per agent and rung.
3. **Eval v1** in `emmet eval`: rungs 2 and 3 beside rung 1 and the photo rung, each with `--dry-run` (the fake
   agents) and `--score <project>` (a hand session; nothing is sent to an agent). Rung 2 scores the track loop's
   closure from its connectors, the platform and canopy, the part count, and its parameters. Rung 3 scores BOM F1
   (part × color) against the set's Rebrickable inventory and placement match against the OMR model's main model. The
   report has a render, the per-run scores, a usage note, and a trend table across the dated reports in
   `docs/evals/`. ADR 0015 records the thresholds after the real runs; any change to one is Barry's.
4. **The flake policy and time budgets in CI**: one retry in E2E only, each retry listed in the job summary; a
   quarantined test carries an issue link or the run fails, and a quarantine is listed in the summary; the Mac jobs
   warn at their budget and fail at 50 % over it.
5. **Nightly**: the corpus job as it is; seven green nights in a row linked in the PR.
