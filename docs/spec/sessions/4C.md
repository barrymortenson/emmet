## Order of work

1. **Profile first.** A 2,000-part stress model (`tests/fixtures/projects/scene-stress`: a town block of ldraw-mini
   parts in many colors, with transparent, chrome, and pearl parts, and real steps), added to `just bench` beside the
   Cottage and the grids, with the frame time during a build animation as a new column. The before numbers go in the
   before/after table below.
2. **Fix the biggest costs** the profile shows, then measure again. Instancing edge cases (a bucket that grows past
   its capacity, a part with lines and no faces), transparent sorting (transparent buckets drawn back to front, after
   the opaque ones), and geometry disposal (nothing left behind after 100 updates: the leak spec).
3. **Materials and lighting.** Every LDraw finish (solid, transparent, chrome, metal, pearl, rubber, glow) from the
   color table, a studio light rig with an environment map for the metals, tone mapping, the optional ground shadow,
   edge and conditional lines tuned for both themes, stud LOD (low-poly studs past a distance), and quality presets
   (Fast, Balanced, Best; picked by the GPU until the user picks one).
4. **Interaction to the design.** Hover (the part's name and color), click to select, box select, Frame, the views,
   the steps (the build up to a step: earlier steps in full color, its new parts outlined, later steps hidden, with
   the parts box and any sub-assembly box over the viewer), layer cut-away, and Screenshot to a file or the
   clipboard. Finding pins at their parts.
5. **Build animation** to section 13: drop and fade in, lift and fade out, glide; Animated, Fast, and Off in Settings;
   Skip and Replay build; reduced motion; renders always of the final state.
6. **Highlight modes** (issue, select, hover, agent), distinct without color alone.
7. **Visual baselines** for six scenes per OS, and the before and after renders.

## Before and after (macOS, this Mac, debug build, `just bench viewer`)

The same scenes and the same stress model, `main`'s viewer (with the bench's draw-cost measure added) against 4C's,
2026-11-01: `docs/spec/sessions/4C/bench-{before,after}-macos.md`. Draw cost is the mean of 60 frames, each drawn and waited
for with a one-pixel read (`drawCost(60)`); the orbit and animation frame times sit at this display's refresh interval (13.9 ms at 72 Hz), so
they hide it.

| Scene | Parts | Draw calls before → after | Draw cost before → after | First load | Slider → viewer | Orbit frame | Build animation frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rung 1 (town, 1 building) | 184 | 87 → 77 | 1.4 → 1.8 ms | 737 → 725 ms | 531 → 546 ms | 13.9 → 13.9 ms | 13.9 → 13.8 ms |
| rung 2 (town, 1 floor) | 478 | 259 → 130 | 2.3 → 2.6 ms | 711 → 731 ms | 631 → 638 ms | 13.9 → 13.9 ms | 13.8 → 13.9 ms |
| Cottage | 905 | 523 → 272 | 3.8 → 2.9 ms | 883 → 850 ms | 731 → 720 ms | 13.9 → 13.9 ms | 13.8 → 13.8 ms |
| 2,000 (town block) | 2,018 | 647 → 229 | 3.4 → 3.0 ms | 718 → 767 ms | 645 → 650 ms | 13.9 → 13.9 ms | 13.8 → 13.8 ms |
| grid 2000 (one part) | 2,000 | 18 → 4 | 2.5 → 2.7 ms | 17 → 40 ms | 643 → 647 ms | 13.9 → 13.9 ms | 13.9 → 13.8 ms |

The biggest cost was draw calls: each (part, color) bucket drew its faces, edges, and conditional lines, so the
Cottage and the town block were at 523 and 647, past section 13's ~300. One bucket per (part, finish) with per-instance
colors brought them to 272 and 229. What 4C adds to a frame (the studio's reflections, the ground shadow's pass at
Best, the outline) costs back some of what the fewer draw calls save: the small models draw a little slower and the
big ones faster. Every budget holds with room at 2,000 parts: first load 767 ms (≤ 6 s), slider → viewer 650 ms
(≤ 3 s), orbit and animation frames at the refresh interval (≤ 25 ms), and `tests/e2e/specs/perf.e2e.mjs` fails over
any of them on the self-hosted Mac.

The stud LOD and Fast's dropped conditional lines are for slower GPUs: on this Mac at Best a stud stays above 2.5 px
in these views, so they don't change these rows. The triangle counts are the same before and after for that reason, but for the ground plane's two.
Windows waits for the Windows pass (D-43).
