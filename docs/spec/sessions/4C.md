## Order of work

1. **Profile first.** A 2,000-part stress model (`tests/fixtures/projects/scene-stress`: a town block of ldraw-mini
   parts in many colors, with transparent, chrome, and pearl parts, and real steps), added to `just bench` beside the
   Cottage and the grids, with the frame time during a build animation as a new column. The before numbers go in the
   before/after table below.
2. **Fix the biggest costs** the profile shows, then measure again. Instancing edge cases (a bucket that grows past
   its capacity, a part with lines and no faces), transparent sorting (transparent buckets drawn back to front, after
   the opaque ones), and geometry disposal (nothing left behind after 100 updates: the leak spec).
3. **Materials and lighting.** Every LDraw finish (solid, transparent, chrome, metal, pearl, rubber, glow) from the
   color table, a studio light rig with an environment map for the metals, tone mapping, the optional ground shadow,
   edge and conditional lines tuned for both themes, stud LOD (low-poly studs past a distance), and quality presets
   (Fast, Balanced, Best; picked by the GPU until the user picks one).
4. **Interaction to the design.** Hover (the part's name and color), click to select, box select, Frame, the views,
   the steps (the build up to a step: earlier steps in full color, its new parts outlined, later steps hidden, with
   the parts box and any sub-assembly box over the viewer), layer cut-away, and Screenshot to a file or the
   clipboard. Finding pins at their parts.
5. **Build animation** to section 13: drop and fade in, lift and fade out, glide; Animated, Fast, and Off in Settings;
   Skip and Replay build; reduced motion; renders always of the final state.
6. **Highlight modes** (issue, select, hover, agent), distinct without color alone.
7. **Visual baselines** for six scenes per OS, and the before and after renders.

## Before and after (macOS, this Mac, debug build)

Before (`just bench viewer` on `main`'s viewer, 2026-11-01; `docs/spec/sessions/4C/bench-before-macos.md`). Draw cost
is a frame drawn and waited for (a one-pixel read), which the display's refresh interval hides from the orbit frame
time on this GPU:

| Scene | Parts | First load | Slider → viewer (median of 6) | Orbit frame | Draw cost | Build animation frame | Draw calls | Triangles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rung 1 (town, 1 building) | 207 | 750 ms | 547 ms | 16.7 ms | 1.6 ms | 16.6 ms (361 frames) | 84 | 156,474 |
| rung 2 (town, 1 floor) | 567 | 765 ms | 647 ms | 16.7 ms | 2.3 ms | 16.6 ms (362 frames) | 251 | 418,145 |
| cottage | 905 | 889 ms | 726 ms | 16.7 ms | 2.9 ms | 16.6 ms (363 frames) | 523 | 498,571 |
| 2,000 (town block) | 2107 | 868 ms | 702 ms | 16.6 ms | 3.8 ms | 16.5 ms (364 frames) | 639 | 1,290,872 |
| grid 2000 | 2000 | 33 ms | 650 ms | 16.7 ms | 2.4 ms | 16.6 ms (362 frames) | 18 | 1,400,000 |

The biggest costs: draw calls (one bucket per part and color, each drawing faces, edges, and conditional lines, so
639 for the town block and 523 for the Cottage, against section 13's ~300) and triangles (studs and the tubes under
bricks, 1.29 million for the town block). The grid shows each alone: 18 draw calls and 1.4 million triangles cost
2.4 ms, so the town block's 621 more draw calls cost about 1.4 ms more here, and more on a slower CPU.
