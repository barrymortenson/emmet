## Shape

One book, two writers' worth of work kept apart:

1. **What to draw** (`emmet_core::instructions`, Rust): from `build/steps.json`, `build/placements.json`, and the BOM,
   the render jobs a book needs: the cover, each step of the main model and of each sub-assembly, and each part × color
   in a parts box or on the BOM page.
2. **Drawing** (the webview, through `viewer.render`): the app's main window, or render mode's hidden window for
   `emmet export --format pdf`, renders every job with one hidden viewer and returns JPEGs plus what the layout needs
   to know about each picture: its crop, the camera's turn, where each new part and each sub-assembly copy is in it,
   whether a new part is hidden or tiny (a zoom box), and the arrows for parts that don't go on from above.
3. **Layout** (Rust): pages from the step rules: the step number under its parts box, the build up to the step with
   its new parts outlined in red, a fixed camera through a run of steps, sub-assembly boxes with numbered sub-steps,
   "2x", and a line to where they go, arrows, zoom boxes, the turn icon, a cover, the BOM pages, and page numbers, on A4
   or Letter. The result is a display list (`Book`).
4. **The PDF** (Rust): the display list written as PDF. The engine is the ADR's decision (below).

The page baselines draw the same display list in the webview and compare it with pixelmatch, per OS. A read-back test
checks the PDF itself.

## Order of work

1. Viewer: `render` options for instructions (a fixed frame, a turn, no shadow, a report on given ids).
2. Layout prototype with rung 1 (the Cottage) to SVG-free pages viewed on screen; Barry sees the first pages.
3. ADR 0014: the PDF engine, measured (file size, fonts, time) on both OSes.
4. The book: cover, steps, sub-assemblies, BOM, page numbers, A4 and Letter; then rung 2.
5. `emmet export --format pdf [--paper a4|letter]` through render mode; Export → Instructions → PDF in the app.
6. Page baselines for rung 1 and rung 2 on macOS and Windows (a manual Windows run, D-43).
7. Barry prints the rung 2 PDF at A4 and Letter.
