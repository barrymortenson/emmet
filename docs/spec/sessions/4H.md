## What it is

In Barry's fifth e-bike session (2026-11-10) the Steps tab listed the battery's and the rack's sub-steps under Built
apart, but a sub-step was a label: clicking it did nothing, and the viewer could only light the whole section in the
model. The PDF shows a section built apart in a framed box with its own numbered steps; the app now shows the same
thing in the viewer.

- **The viewer** gets `showAssembly(copy, upTo)`: one copy of a sub-assembly on its own, where it sits in the model,
  built up to one of its own steps with that step's new parts outlined. Every other part is hidden (not unloaded), as
  later steps are. A part's step inside the copy is its own step, or for a sub-assembly nested in it, the step that
  places that copy (what `subScene` does for the PDF's pictures). Starting it remembers where the camera was and frames
  the whole copy, from the side the camera was already on, so the camera stays put from sub-step to sub-step as the
  PDF's does; `showAssembly(null)` shows the model again and puts the camera back. The cut-away and the ground grid
  are the whole model's, so they wait while a sub-assembly shows. Renders for the agent still draw the whole model.
- **The Steps tab**: a sub-step is a tree item that can be clicked (or picked with Enter). It shows the first copy of
  its sub-assembly. `[` and `]` then move through that sub-assembly's steps. A main step, Escape, or the
  sub-assembly's own row ends it; the row then lights every copy in the model, as it does today. A new build, a
  version opened to compare, Replay build, and another project end it too.
- **Over the viewer**: the sub-assembly's frame (the PDF's framed box) around the sub-step's number and parts box,
  with the sub-assembly's name and its "5x" when it's built more than once. Design change note 008 records it.
