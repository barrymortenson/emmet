## Order of work

1. **The baseline.** `emmet eval` learns the five technique prompts (`docs/spec/ladder.md`, "Technique prompts") and
   keeps each run's project, so the baseline can be checked again once `figure_clearance` exists. The ten baseline runs
   (five prompts, both agents) use `main`'s library, doctrine, and validator: the eval change is the only change.
2. **Building rules** (`docs/spec/building-rules.md`), from the guides, the book notes by set number (D-48), and
   general building knowledge, each tagged with where it's enforced. Barry approves them (H-27) before step 3.
3. **`figure_clearance`** (D-53): `m.keep_clear(box, why)` in the DSL, door frames' keep-clear boxes from the parts
   index, the rule in `emmet-core/src/check/`, `docs/spec/validator.md`, section 08's API and section 11's rule table,
   `emmet api`, the `broken-figure-clearance` golden project, and the mutations.
4. **The audit** (`docs/techniques/audit.md`): every technique, its figure-facing sizes, what it doesn't let the caller
   change or doesn't return, a verdict, and the fix in the technique.
5. **Doctrine:** the rules the validator can't check, in `figure-scale.md`, `large.md`, `vehicles.md`, and step 5 of
   EMMET.md's loop.
6. **The end runs** and the report, `docs/evals/<date>-technique.md`; Barry judges the before and after renders (H-27).
