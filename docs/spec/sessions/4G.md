## What the e-bike retry ran into

Barry's retry (2026-11-04, 426 parts) placed every beam, pin, axle, and wheel with `add_raw` and hand-built LDraw
matrices, after test builds to learn which way a beam's holes face. With its `m.allow` lines taken out, the model
reports:

- `collision`: each wheel (88517c01) and the axle through its hub. The axle seats in the hub's axle hole, but the seat
  exemption covers only the 20 LDU where the two meet, and the axle runs on through the hub's bore.
- `weak_connection`: each wheel "is held by 1 stud": an axle's seat has a stud's radius (6 LDU), so the rule took it
  for a stud.

The chain (3711 links on 4019 and 3648b) already validates; the retry's allows for it were not needed.

## Order of work

1. **Connection points.** `emmet_core::technic`: every Technic hole, pin, and axle of a part from its connectors (the
   LDCad data, the primitive table, the overrides): pin holes, axle holes, pins, axles, and axle pins, each with its
   middle, its axis, and its length. Named `1` to `n` in the order of their middles in the DSL frame at `rot=0` (x,
   then y, then z). `part_info` gives them in studs and plates from the footprint corner at `rot=0`; `emmet part`
   and the MCP text say them in words.
2. **The DSL.** The points go to the model in `build/part-geometry.json`. `Handle.hole(name)` and `Handle.holes` give a
   placed part's points in the model's frame; `m.add(..., hole=n)` places a part by one of its holes;
   `m.beam(part, color, at, run, rise, axis="y", holes=(1, None))` lays a beam (or any part with two holes on one
   axis) from one hole to another on a triangle, and says which triangles land on holes when the run and rise don't;
   `m.insert(part, color, hole, out="+y", out_by=None)` seats a pin, axle, or axle pin in a named hole.
3. **The validator.** A pin or axle seated in a hole exempts its whole length inside that part from `collision` (an
   axle through a hub); `weak_connection` counts studs only, so a part on a pin or axle isn't "held by 1 stud"; a pin
   left out of its hole is a finding that names the hole it missed.
4. **Display-vehicle techniques**, each with a render, a provenance note, its parts in ldraw-mini, and a test like 3C's:
   `motorbike_frame`, `suspension_fork`, `swingarm_shock`, `chain_drive`, `disc_brake`, `handlebars`, and
   `display_stand_nameplate`, on a review page for Barry.
5. **The large guide's "Display bikes"** section beside "Display cars"; the doctrine points Technic builds at `part_info`'s
   holes and the new calls.
6. Barry's hand session: the photo rung's e-bike in the app, scored with `emmet eval --score`.
